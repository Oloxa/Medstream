Robotic-Assisted Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy: Critical View of Safety & Calot's Dissection

Primary Specialty: Minimally Invasive General Surgery
Accreditation: ACS / SAGES Verified CME
Lead Surgeons: Dr. Katherine Vance, MD, FACS; Dr. Liam Zhang, MD
Institution / Center: Johns Hopkins Medicine (USA)
Clinical Director: Prof. Alexander Sterling, MD, FRCSEd
Release Year: 2026
Audio & Commentary: English (Surgeon Dual-Track Voiceover)
Archive Views (Hits): 312,400 Verified Clinical Views
This masterclass details the complete robotic surgical technique for cholecystectomy in high-BMI patients with severe chronic cholecystitis and dense fibrotic adhesions. Key operative phases include establishing a 4-port da Vinci Xi geometry, traction on Hartmann's pouch, rigorous anterior and posterior peritoneal scoring, clearance of fat from the hepatocystic triangle (Calot's triangle), and formal demonstration of Strasberg's Critical View of Safety (CVS) with dual-structure clearance. Additional modules cover intraoperative indocyanine green (ICG) fluorescence cholangiography, hem-o-lok clipping, liver bed electrocautery, and endobag specimen retrieval without peritoneal spillage.
